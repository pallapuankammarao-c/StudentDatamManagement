Public Live Dashboard Link

Anyone with this link can open and view the live student dashboard, performance analytics, and records directly in their browser.

How to share with others on the internet or local Wi-Fi:
  • On the same Wi-Fi / Local Network: Share your machine's IP address (e.g. http://<your-local-ip>:5000).
  • Publicly over the Internet: Run a free Cloudflare Tunnel or ngrok to get a secure public HTTPS link that anyone worldwide can open!
  • Check the Settings & Sharing page for 1-click terminal commands.
